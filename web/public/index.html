chordclean

Tab-site PDFs, stripped down to chords over lyrics: a lead sheet for piano, voice, or guitar.

Drop a tab PDF here

or choose a file


  






  

    Everything happens in your browser: your PDF is never uploaded, stored,
    or logged, and nothing about it is kept when you close the tab.
    chordclean counts how many sheets it has cleaned, and nothing else.
  

  

    Provided as-is, with absolutely no warranty. Bring your own PDF; chordclean
    only reformats a file you already have.
